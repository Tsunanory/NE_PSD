## Corrected invariants

- A copied PSD or PSB parent may change during relink/embed only when the operation acknowledges the exact copied parent and source/target paths. The adapter independently hashes copied bytes, rereads the linked closure, verifies every copied child against source bytes, and rehashes all originals. A changed parent with a void or mismatched reply fails.
- Qualification rejects symlinked evidence roots, parents, and leaves, including symlinks that point back inside the approved root. A missing leaf is accepted only under a real approved parent. Ambiguous mutation replies skip transaction and copy cleanup even when persisting the recovery barrier fails.
- Recovery reads a nonempty, identity-matching persisted journal. Unknown operations require idle and safe-completion proof, pending transport clearance, durable `reconciled` records, and a fresh journal reread before closing owned documents. Empty, missing, mismatched, corrupt, or still-pending journals cannot prove safe recovery.
- Successful normal operations, recovery, and explicit reconciliation require `release()` to report `released: true`; a retained installation lock cannot be reported as success.
